# AI Support Agent — marketing brief for growthable.io

**Audience:** the marketing team. Everything in here is accurate to what
shipped as of 2026-08-08 — claims beyond this file need a product check
first. Where a claim is off-limits, it's called out explicitly, with the
line to use instead.

---

## What it is, in one sentence

A chat bubble on your Growthable help centre that answers your clients'
GoHighLevel questions instantly, around the clock — switched on with one
click, no code, no setup.

## The story to tell

Agency owners drown in the same GoHighLevel questions every week —
"why won't my workflow re-enrol a contact", "where do I connect my
domain", "how do I set up A2P". Every one interrupts billable work.

The pitch: **your help centre now answers back.** Growthable customers
already have a help centre stocked with every published GoHighLevel
article. This adds an AI assistant that reads all of it — about 24,000
indexed passages — and answers conversationally, in any language the
client writes in, at 3am on a Sunday.

And it proves its own value: every week the owner gets an email with the
number of conversations handled and the hours of support time saved.
The product does the selling; that's the design.

## How it works (customer's view)

1. In the Growthable dashboard, click **Add AI chat widget**. That's the
   whole install — the widget appears on their help centre automatically.
2. The same click sets up their **client portal**: satisfaction scores,
   AI analysis of what clients are asking, per-sub-account controls, and
   the weekly report email. One button signs them in — no password setup.
3. They can restyle it (colours, logo, greeting, position) in a visual
   customiser, and paste one snippet to run the same assistant inside
   their HighLevel agency.
4. Free for 7 days, full portal included. Upgrading happens self-serve
   inside the product — no sales call, no demo booking.

## Feature bullets (safe to use)

- Answers your clients' GoHighLevel questions instantly, day or night
- Reads the full public GoHighLevel help centre — ~24,000 indexed passages
- Says so honestly when it doesn't know, rather than inventing an answer
- Replies in your client's language automatically — Spanish in, Spanish out
- Live in one click on your Growthable help centre; one snippet for anywhere else
- Client portal included: CSAT scores, AI topic analysis, per-sub-account on/off
- A weekly email that counts the conversations handled and the hours saved
- Match it to your brand: colours, logo, greeting, launcher position
- 7-day free trial with the full portal — upgrade only if the numbers convince you

## Numbers for copy

- **~24,000** indexed help-centre passages behind every answer
- **7 minutes** of support time credited per AI-handled conversation —
  this is the basis of every "time saved" figure the product shows, so
  marketing copy using time-saved maths must use the same rate
- **7-day** free trial
- Example arithmetic that's safe because it's the product's own: *"40
  questions answered while you slept is roughly 4½ hours you didn't
  spend typing the same reply."*

## Claims you MUST NOT make

| Don't say | Say instead | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "Trained on your business" / "knows your account" | "Answers your clients' GoHighLevel questions" | The knowledge base is the public GoHighLevel help centre. Per-customer content is a planned follow-up, not shipped. |
| "Hands off to your team when it's stuck" (in trial/free context) | "Tells your clients honestly when it doesn't know" | Human handoff is a paid-plan feature. Trial widgets are AI-only. |
| "Your trial converts automatically" | "Upgrade in a couple of minutes if you like the numbers" | Trials never auto-convert; the customer must actively pick a plan. |
| "White-label portal" (as a trial feature) | "On a paid plan, the portal runs on your own domain" | Custom DNS is paid-only. |
| Uptime/SLA figures, response-time guarantees | "answers instantly", unquantified | No SLA exists. |

## The upgrade hooks (what paid adds)

Product-led means the trial IS the demo. Paid adds, in the order they
bite:

1. **The weekly report keeps coming.** When the trial lapses, the report
   email becomes a teaser: headline numbers stay, the breakdown locks.
   The widget keeps answering — we never punish their clients.
2. **Human handoff.** Paid widgets can offer a real person and route
   conversations to the owner's team; trial widgets are AI-only.
3. **White-label.** The portal on the agency's own domain.

## Suggested page structure

1. **Hero:** "Your help centre now answers back." Sub: "An AI assistant
   that resolves your clients' GoHighLevel questions instantly — on the
   help centre you already have." CTA: "Add it in one click — free for 7
   days".
2. **Proof strip:** the weekly-report framing — screenshot of the email
   ("23 chats handled, ~2h 40m saved") beats any testimonial at launch.
3. **How it works:** the three steps above, screenshots of the dashboard
   button, the widget answering, the portal.
4. **For agencies:** per-sub-account control + the HighLevel snippet —
   one assistant across the whole client footprint.
5. **Honesty block:** "When it doesn't know, it says so." Position the
   no-hallucination behaviour as a feature — agency owners have been
   burned by chatbots that invent answers.
6. **Pricing:** free 7-day trial, then self-serve upgrade. No sales-call
   CTA anywhere on the page.

## FAQ drafts

**Do I need to install anything?**
No. One click in your Growthable dashboard and it's live on your help
centre. There's an embed snippet if you also want it inside your
HighLevel agency.

**What does it know?**
The entire public GoHighLevel help centre — every published article,
indexed and searched on every question. It doesn't have access to your
account or your clients' data, and it won't pretend to.

**What happens when it can't answer?**
It says so, plainly. It never invents features, prices, or URLs. On paid
plans it can offer to bring in a member of your team.

**What happens when the trial ends?**
The widget keeps answering your clients. The portal and the weekly
reports pause until you upgrade — nothing is deleted, and everything
picks up where it left off.

**Can my clients tell it's AI?**
The widget is honest about being an assistant, and you control its name,
look, and greeting.
